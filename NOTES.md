The first release of Chair, a free appointment diary for hairdressers on Android.

- Day, week and list views for every stylist, with processing time left free for other bookings.
- Time off blanked out properly: holidays, breaks, training and closures, repeating blocks, and UK bank holidays worked out for you.
- Client cards with colour formulas, skin tests and before and after photos.
- Reminders by text or WhatsApp, repeat bookings, rebooking and a waiting list that fills gaps.
- Takings with a PDF report for your accountant, home visits, three widgets and password-locked backups.
- No adverts, no account, no monthly fee. It cannot go online.

Android 8 or later. Full notes: https://customgpter.github.io/chair/releases.html
