Release notes

What changed in each version of Chair. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.0.0 21 November 2026Latest

  • First release. The diary: day, week and list views for every stylist, side-by-side lanes for overlapping bookings, drag to move, stretch to lengthen, and a line for the time now.
  • Services with work, processing and clean-down time, so developing time stays free for other bookings. A ready-made starter menu of cuts, colours and treatments.
  • Stylists with their own colours, working days, hours and one-off changes.
  • Time off: holidays, days off, breaks, training, personal time and salon closures, for one stylist or everyone, repeating every working day, week or fortnight with dates skipped. Bookings in the way are listed with a message for each client.
  • UK bank holidays for England and Wales, Scotland and Northern Ireland, worked out for any year, with substitute days. Open on one with a tap.
  • Clash warnings, free times for any day, and the next free slot for chosen services.
  • Reminders: ready-written texts and WhatsApp messages in your own words, a nudge the evening before, and optional automatic texts from your phone.
  • Clients: notes, alerts, visit history, spend, how often they return, no-shows and late cancellations, home addresses and quick call, text and WhatsApp.
  • Colour formulas with copy from last time, skin tests with 48-hour and expiry warnings and reaction alerts, and encrypted before and after photos.
  • Repeat bookings every 3 to 12 weeks, a rebook prompt after payment, and a list of clients due back.
  • A waiting list matched to free gaps, with offer messages written for you.
  • Takings: payments by card, cash, transfer or other, tips and products, totals and charts by day, week, month or year, stylist and service breakdowns, how full the diary was and what no-shows cost.
  • A PDF takings report and spreadsheets of takings, bookings and clients.
  • Home visits with travel time either side.
  • Three widgets: today's list, now and next, and quick actions.
  • A copy of the diary in your phone's calendar, with client names hidden if you wish.
  • Import clients from a spreadsheet or your phone's contacts, choosing who to add and skipping anyone already in Chair.
  • Data protection tools: a client's full record to give them, forget a client, find clients not seen for years, and a privacy notice.
  • Backup: a password-locked backup file including photos, a daily copy to a folder you choose keeping the newest seven, and restore with undo.
  • App lock with a PIN or fingerprint, light and dark themes, and a 12 or 24-hour clock.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.0.0 is called Chair.apk. It is 2.4 MB (2,536,510 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

04bbc4efb6551df23be9a978dc9ed830d2bbc26833ee6a6b3c9ec852ad78af4b

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Chair.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.
